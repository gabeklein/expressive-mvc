First release. `expressive dev` and `expressive build`: file-based routes from `app/` on `@expressive/dom` and `@expressive/router`, `remote.ts` sidecars served through `@expressive/rpc`, server Models seated per route folder, and a Node service in `dist/server`. Twins - server Models mirrored on the client - are experimental.

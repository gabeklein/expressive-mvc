First release. `class X extends Steps` makes a Model runnable: every method is a step, a step schedules the next by calling it, and fields carry data between them. `run(...args)` is the entry - it schedules, `await this` waits for the run, and its return is the typed result of `x.run()` and `await x`. Steps compose into sequences, parallel groups, mapped groups, durations (`this.then({ min: 1 })`) and waits answered from outside or by a racing step - losing racers are unwound; each step is atomic and registers handlers for its subtree's outcomes - `this.then.on({ reset, catch, abort, close })` - `abort` given why a subtree ended without completing (an error, a `Steps.Aborted` or `Steps.Stopped`), `close` a finally - so a failure unwinds what committed, a step can recover what it scheduled, and a step owning a resource settles it on any ending. `.catch(handler)` recovers a branch, and a handler in `then.on` may be a step method too - a step method is recorded, given the error or reason, a function stays process-local, and `this.then.retry()` replays the failed step or group with its args; `this.then.only(max, key?)` is a gate for concurrency, `this.then.commit()` marks a point of no return, `this.then.stop()` halts and `this.then.abort()` halts and unwinds. Runs repeat and queue; status, queue and waits are reactive on `this.then`. In-process only - no durable persistence yet.
